North Indian diet plan for weight loss

You do not have to give up roti, dal or rajma to lose weight. North Indian food is already built on some of the best cheap protein in the country — dal, rajma, chole, paneer, curd. What usually goes wrong is the ghee, the second paratha and the portion of rice, not the food itself.

What makes the difference in North Indian food

  • Watch the ghee, not the dalA teaspoon of ghee or butter is roughly 45 kcal, and a tadka or a buttered roti can carry two or three. That is where a home-cooked North Indian day quietly gains 200–300 kcal.
  • Roti or rice is a portion questionOne roti is about 120 kcal and a cup of cooked rice about 205 kcal. Two rotis and one cup of rice are close — how many you eat matters far more than which.
  • Lean on rajma, chole and dalA cup of cooked dal is around 18 g of protein, rajma 13 g, chole 13 g. Make one of them the centre of lunch and dinner and protein mostly takes care of itself.
  • Thin lassi, not sweet lassiSweet lassi carries a lot of sugar. Salted lassi or chaas (about 60 kcal a glass) gives you the curd without it.
  • Paratha is a weekend foodA stuffed paratha cooked in ghee can be two to three times a plain roti. Keep it to breakfast a couple of days a week and pair it with curd.

How the numbers are worked out

Your daily calorie need is estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, adjusted for activity. For weight loss we subtract 500 kcal a day — roughly 0.45 kg a week — and never go below 1,500 kcal for men or 1,200 kcal for women. Protein is set at 1.8 g per kg of body weight, using an adjusted weight above a BMI of 25 so the target stays realistic. Every calorie figure is an approximation of a typical home-cooked portion.

Questions people ask

Can I lose weight eating North Indian food?

Yes. Weight loss comes from eating somewhat fewer calories than you burn, consistently — not from a particular cuisine. Keep the North Indian meals you like, control the portion of rice or roti and the cooking fat, and get enough protein.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

It depends on your age, height, weight, sex and how active you are. The free planner works it out with the Mifflin-St Jeor formula and subtracts 500 kcal a day, which is roughly 0.45 kg a week. It never goes below 1,500 kcal for men or 1,200 kcal for women.

Is roti better than rice for weight loss?

Neither is "better". One roti is about 120 kcal and a cup of cooked rice about 205 kcal, so two rotis and a cup of rice land in the same place. Pick the one you enjoy and control how much of it you eat.
