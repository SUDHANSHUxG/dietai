How to make 2,000 kcal work

  • Adjust the grains, keep the dalA roti is about 120 kcal and a cup of rice about 205 kcal. Moving the rice or roti up or down is the easiest way to hit a number without changing what you eat.
  • Count the cooking fatA teaspoon of oil or ghee is roughly 45 kcal. A heavy tadka can add 100–200 kcal that no recipe card shows.
  • Put protein at every mealDal, curd, paneer, chana, eggs, chicken or fish at each meal makes it easier to reach your protein goal inside 2,000 kcal.

Questions people ask

Is 2,000 calories a day right for me?

It depends on your age, height, weight, sex and how active you are, so 2,000 is only a starting point. The free planner works out your own number with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation. It never sets less than 1,200 kcal for women or 1,500 kcal for men.

Can I eat roti and rice at 2,000 calories?

Yes. One roti is about 120 kcal and a cup of cooked rice about 205 kcal, so at 2,000 kcal it is the portion that matters, not the food. The sample days above include both.

How much protein do these vegetarian days give?

The two vegetarian days above give about 71–90 g of protein. The planner sets your own protein goal from your weight and, where the meals fall short, suggests a top-up — soya chunks, paneer, curd or eggs — counted inside your calories.
