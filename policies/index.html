Terms, privacy and delivery

Diet AI (dietai.in) gives free Indian diet plans, guides and a food tracker, plus an optional 7-day plan pack for a one-time ₹99. There is no account and no subscription. Last updated 15 November 2026.

Terms of use

  • Estimates, not medical adviceEverything on Diet AI is a general nutrition estimate for education. Calorie and protein figures are approximations. It is not meant for anyone under 18, or for anyone who is pregnant or managing a health condition without their doctor's or dietitian's advice.
  • Your plan, for your usePlans are built on your device from what you enter. The 7-day pack is for your personal use.
  • ChangesDiet AI may change, improve or stop features. The pages and plans are provided as they are.

Pricing

  • ₹99, one timeThe 7-day plan pack costs ₹99, charged once in Indian rupees through Razorpay. The planner, guides and tracker are free.

Privacy

  • What stays on your phoneWhat you type into the planner and tracker — age, height, weight, plans, food log, water and weight history — is kept in your browser's storage on your device and is not sent to Diet AI.
  • What the site countsTo see which pages and features people use, the site sends anonymous events to its server: for example a page view, that a plan was made (with its region, diet, goal and calorie target), that checkout was opened, or that the page hit an error (the error message and where in the code it happened). Each event carries where you first came from, such as a search engine or a link's source tag, but no name, email, phone number, age, height or weight. Diet AI itself sets no cookies.
  • PaymentsPayments are handled by Razorpay, under Razorpay's own terms and privacy policy. Diet AI never sees your card, UPI or bank details. Its server receives the payment ID, amount and status from Razorpay to confirm the purchase, and keeps the payment ID and amount to count sales.
  • Your unlockAfter you pay, an unlock code is saved in your browser so the pack stays open. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes it; "Already paid?" brings it back.

Delivery

  • Digital, nothing shippedThe 7-day pack is a digital plan. It appears on this site as soon as Razorpay confirms your payment, usually within seconds.
  • If it doesn't openIf your phone closed the page during payment, or you are on a new phone, tap "Already paid?" and enter the payment ID from your Razorpay receipt — it starts with pay_. This works on any device.
