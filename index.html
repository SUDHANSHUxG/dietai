Questions people ask

Is it really free?
Yes. The planner, tracker, water and weight logs are free forever. The optional Premium Pack (₹99 one-time) adds a full 7-day region-matched plan with a grocery list.
Will I have to eat boiled chicken and salads?
No. Plans are built from your region's everyday meals — dal-roti, idli-sambar, macher jhol, thepla. Fat loss comes from portions and consistency, not exotic food.
Where is my data stored?
Your age, weight, height, plans, food log, water and weight history are stored only in your own browser and are never uploaded — we couldn't see them even if we wanted to. The one thing we do collect is anonymous usage counts (page views, which buttons get clicked, roughly where visitors came from) so we know which parts of the site are worth improving. No names, no personal data, no advertising trackers. If you buy the Premium Pack, Razorpay handles the payment; we keep only the payment ID so your purchase can be confirmed and restored.
How fast will I lose weight?
The plan sets your calories about 500 kcal a day below your estimated maintenance. On paper that is roughly 0.45 kg a week, but real results vary from person to person and usually slow down over time. Slower is fine; consistency matters more than speed.
