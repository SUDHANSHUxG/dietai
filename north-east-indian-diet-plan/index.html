North-East Indian diet plan for weight loss

Food across the North-East is often boiled, steamed or fermented, with little oil — which already makes it one of the easiest cuisines in India to lose weight on. The rice portion and the fried versions of dishes are the things to keep an eye on.

What makes the difference in North-East Indian food

  • It is already leanBoiled greens, steamed fish and bamboo-shoot curries are low in oil. Keep cooking the way you already do.
  • Steamed momos, not friedSix steamed veg momos are about 320 kcal. Fried momos are far more.
  • Thukpa is a full mealA big bowl of thukpa with chicken or tofu covers carbohydrate, vegetables and protein in one dish.
  • Portion the sticky riceSticky rice is dense; half a cup goes further than it looks.
  • Fish and greensRiver fish with lai xaak or other greens is about as good as a weight-loss lunch gets.

How the numbers are worked out

Your daily calorie need is estimated with the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, adjusted for activity. For weight loss we subtract 500 kcal a day — roughly 0.45 kg a week — and never go below 1,500 kcal for men or 1,200 kcal for women. Protein is set at 1.8 g per kg of body weight, using an adjusted weight above a BMI of 25 so the target stays realistic. Every calorie figure is an approximation of a typical home-cooked portion.

Questions people ask

Can I lose weight eating North-East Indian food?

Yes. Weight loss comes from eating somewhat fewer calories than you burn, consistently — not from a particular cuisine. Keep the North-East Indian meals you like, control the portion of rice or roti and the cooking fat, and get enough protein.

How many calories should I eat to lose weight?

It depends on your age, height, weight, sex and how active you are. The free planner works it out with the Mifflin-St Jeor formula and subtracts 500 kcal a day, which is roughly 0.45 kg a week. It never goes below 1,500 kcal for men or 1,200 kcal for women.

Are momos healthy for weight loss?

Steamed momos can fit: six steamed veg momos are about 320 kcal with 10 g of protein. Choose steamed over fried, and go easy on the mayo.
